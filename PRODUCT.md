# Radio AI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16, React 19 y TypeScript, según la especificación facilitada.

Administration interface for the owner of an autonomous Spanish music station. Its purpose is to keep the audio on air and make operational decisions visible. It is used frequently on desktop and occasionally on mobile.

Product truth comes from docs/SPEC_RADIO_AI.md. Continuous streaming is provided by Liquidsoap and Icecast. AI enriches programming; it never supplies the continuity path. Missing services and missing content must remain visible. All generated voice is disclosed as AI.

The supplied brief fixes a light interface, Geist typography, discrete sidebar, tables, timelines and contextual actions. No decorative analytics and no invented listener counts. Assumption delegated by the user's autonomous request: accent is restrained green and local administrative access uses an environment-token session.
